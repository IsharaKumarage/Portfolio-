What I Learned in PPW

A reflective journal of key skills and insights gained throughout the Professional and Personal Writing module.

01
✏️
Business Writing

I learned how to write professional documents such as proposals, memos, and executive summaries. This improved my ability to organize ideas logically, use the right tone, and format content for business and academic use.

02
✉️
Letter Writing

I gained knowledge on the structure and tone of different types of letters including cover letters, inquiry letters, and thank-you notes. This helped me write clear, polite, and well-structured letters for professionals and organizations.

03
📊
Report Writing

I learned how to collect, analyze, and present information in a clear and organized way. This includes structuring reports, using data effectively, and summarizing key findings to support decisions and project outcomes.

04
📧
Email Writing

I developed skills in writing professional emails with appropriate subject lines, greetings, and tone. I also learned email etiquette and how to communicate respectfully and efficiently in academic and workplace environments.

05
🎤
Language for Presentations

I improved my public speaking and presentation skills by learning how to present ideas clearly and confidently. I learned to structure content, use visuals effectively, and engage the audience while maintaining a professional delivery style.

06
🧠
Personal Reflection

I became more aware of my communication strengths and areas for improvement. This helped me build confidence, improve self-awareness, and handle communication challenges in both academic projects and real-world situations.
