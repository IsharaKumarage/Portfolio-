Career Plan

My Professional Roadmap

A clear roadmap to achieve my professional goals and make a meaningful impact in the field of UI/UX and design.

🎯
Short Term
0 – 1 Year

Build a strong foundation, gain practical experience, and grow as a creative professional.

  • Strengthen my skills in UI/UX design, visual design, and prototyping tools.
  • Work on real-world projects to build a strong portfolio.
  • Learn and stay updated with the latest design trends and technologies.
  • Improve my communication and collaboration skills in a team environment.
📈
Mid Term
1 – 3 Years

Expand my expertise, take on greater responsibilities, and contribute to impactful design solutions.

  • Secure a full-time role as a UI/UX or Graphic Designer.
  • Work on diverse projects that solve real user problems.
  • Enhance my skills in user research, interaction design, and design systems.
  • Take on leadership opportunities in projects and mentor junior designers.
🏔️
Long Term
3 – 5+ Years

Achieve leadership, create meaningful impact, and build a strong personal brand.

  • Become a lead designer or design manager driving user-centered solutions.
  • Build and grow my personal brand through sharing knowledge and case studies.
  • Work on innovative projects that create a positive impact.
  • Continuously learn, adapt, and explore new opportunities in design and technology.
